# Stage 05 evaluation corpus: source candidates and rights

Research note for [Define the representative architecture evaluation corpus and gates](https://github.com/haitham113/pdfMining/issues/3). Checked 2026-10-06. These are **candidate inputs**, not a finalized corpus or a claim that each PDF fits the MVP upload limit. The [Stage 04 SRS](../Stage%2004%20-%20PdfMining%20Software%20Requirements%20Specification%20v0.1.md) sets a 20-page initial maximum and an approximately 25 MB configurable target. File characteristics, selectable text, and page geometry still need inspection before assignment to any evaluation cell.

## Recommended construction of the accepted 9 + 6 set

- Build the nine **native/scanned/mixed × Arabic/English/mixed-language** core PDFs from original, purpose-written content, or permission-cleared short excerpts. Derive scanned and mixed-page variants from the *same* source content to control the effect of extraction mode. Record a manifest with original source, license/permission, transformation, language and page modalities, page count, bytes, known text, reading order, and source boxes. A mixed-language cell must contain Arabic and English on a relevant page, rather than merely being a report offered in two editions.
- Use real publisher PDFs above as **external-validity checks** within the 20-page and active byte limits, after inspecting their actual modality and rights. Paired language editions are useful for comparable questions but are not, by themselves, mixed-language PDFs. Do not assume a downloaded PDF is redistributable merely because it is publicly readable.
- Make six independent stress cases from owned source material: page rotation, nondefault crop, difficult reading order, a native/scanned table case, degraded OCR, and conflicting figures. Keep any additional multi-page table or chart case identifiable rather than hiding it in a general table score. Each case needs known page/region truth and an expected degraded or abstention outcome where applicable.
- Keep private, permission-cleared real documents outside Git and in a separate restricted evaluation set. Record consent and allowed uses before ingesting them. Avoid including personal or sensitive content in hosted AI-provider evaluations unless the later external-processing boundary explicitly permits it.

## Open checks before selection

1. Confirm the language, page count, bytes, selectable text, and coordinates in each candidate PDF. Search snippets and metadata do not establish extraction mode.
2. Review the exact pages used from World Bank and WHO reports for third-party credits. Follow attribution, share-alike, and noncommercial conditions as applicable. For open public portfolio fixtures, purpose-made originals offer the cleanest rights position.
3. Confirm the final corpus has modern Arabic and mixed-language professional layouts; the historical public-domain scans cannot stand in for them.
4. Create the annotated fixture manifest only after the evaluation-design decision specifies what constitutes a gold answer, source span/region, table cell, and expected warning/abstention.
